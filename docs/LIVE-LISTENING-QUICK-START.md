# 🎧 Live Listening - Quick Start Guide

## For Hosts (Starting a Session)

### Step 1: Play Some Music
1. Load a playlist in Track Miner
2. Click play on any track
3. The mini-player will appear at the bottom

### Step 2: Start Live Session
1. Look for the **Radio icon** (📻) in the mini-player
2. Click it to open the "Start Live Listening" dialog
3. Enter your name (this will be displayed to listeners)
4. Click **"Start Session"**

### Step 3: Share the Link
1. The share dialog will automatically open
2. You'll see a unique room link (e.g., `trackminer.app/live/1760308000000-xyz123`)
3. Share it with friends using:
   - **Copy Link** button (then paste in chat)
   - **WhatsApp** button (opens WhatsApp with pre-filled message)
   - **Telegram** button (opens Telegram share)
   - **Messenger** button (opens Facebook Messenger)

### Step 4: Control the Music
1. Play music normally - it syncs automatically to all listeners
2. See the listener count badge on the Radio icon
3. Click the Radio icon anytime to manage the session
4. Click **"End Session"** to stop the live listening

## For Listeners (Joining a Session)

### Step 1: Click the Link
1. Friend sends you a link like: `https://trackminer.app/live/1760308000000-xyz123`
2. Click it to open in your browser

### Step 2: Auto-Join
1. You'll automatically join the session
2. See: "🎧 Listening Live with [Host Name]"
3. Music will start playing in sync

### Step 3: Listen Together
1. Hear exactly what the host is playing
2. Adjust your own volume as needed
3. See the current listener count
4. Watch for "🔄 Syncing..." when tracks change

### Step 4: Leave When Done
1. Click **"Leave Session"** button
2. Or just close the tab

## Visual Indicators

### Host View
- **Gray Radio Icon**: Not hosting (click to start)
- **Green Pulsing Radio Icon**: Active session
- **Number Badge**: Current listener count

### Listener View
- **Purple/Blue Gradient Background**: Immersive experience
- **Large Album Art**: Current track display
- **"🔄 Syncing..." Message**: Updates in progress
- **Disabled Controls**: Only host can control playback
- **Active Volume Slider**: You control your own volume

## Tips & Tricks

### For Hosts
- 🎵 **Change tracks freely** - listeners sync automatically
- 👥 **Monitor listeners** - see count in real-time
- 🔄 **Session persists** - even if you change tabs (as long as music plays)
- 🛑 **End cleanly** - use "End Session" button to notify everyone

### For Listeners
- 🔊 **Independent Volume** - adjust without affecting others
- 🔄 **Auto-Sync** - updates every 5 seconds + on track change
- 📱 **Mobile Friendly** - works on phones and tablets
- 🌐 **Browser Only** - no app download needed

## Troubleshooting

### "Room not found" Error
- The host may have ended the session
- Ask for a new link

### Music Not Playing
- Check if you have muted your browser/device
- Try adjusting the volume slider
- Refresh the page

### Out of Sync
- Wait 5 seconds - auto-sync will fix it
- If persistent, try leaving and rejoining

### Can't Hear Anything
- Make sure your device volume is up
- Check the in-app volume slider
- Some browsers block autoplay - click play if needed
